Hello, I'm Shivani

The subjects change. What I look for stays the same.

I have loved drawing and painting for as long as I can remember.

I hold a BFA and MFA in Fine Arts and have been working as an artist for more than 10+ years. I am also currently undertaking advanced academic art training through an Atelier programme.

Over the years, I have worked on everything from studio paintings and portraits to pet art, spiritual pieces, abstract work, and live weddings. The subjects change, but what I look for stays the same, something that makes the painting feel like it belongs to the person it was created for.
