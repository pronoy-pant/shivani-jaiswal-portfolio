● LIVE PAINTING · AT THE VENUE · ACRYLIC

Live Painting, while the moment is still happening

There is something very different about watching a blank canvas slowly become part of a celebration. For live weddings, I arrive before the main moment, set up at the venue, and begin building the scene. As the celebration unfolds, the people, colours, and details are added to the canvas. By the end of the event, the painting is complete and ready for its reveal.

● THE ARTIST · IN THE STUDIO

Hello, I’m Shivani

I have loved drawing and painting for as long as I can remember.

I hold a BFA and MFA in Fine Arts and have been working as an artist for more than 10 years. I am also currently undertaking advanced academic art training through an Atelier programme.

Over the years, I have worked on everything from studio paintings and portraits to pet art, spiritual pieces, abstract work, and live weddings.

The subjects change, but what I look for stays the same, something that makes the painting feel like it belongs to the person it was created for.
