## What Happens Next?
I personally read every enquiry.
I usually reply within 48 hours.
If you are enquiring about a wedding, I will first check whether your date is available.
If the date works, we can have a short call to talk about your wedding, venue, painting idea, and the next steps.
If I am not available on your date, I will let you know as soon as possible.

## Planning a Wedding for Your Client?
I also work with wedding planners and event teams.
If you are planning a wedding and would like to include live painting, send me the date, city, venue, and ceremony details.
I can share availability, painting options, and what I need at the venue.
### What I Need at the Venue
A good light for painting
One sturdy table
One chair
Enough space to set up comfortably
A fan or cooler for hot outdoor events
A heater if the weather is cold
I can also coordinate with the planner or photography team before the wedding so the setup is smooth on the day.
