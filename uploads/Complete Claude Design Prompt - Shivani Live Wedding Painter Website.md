# DESIGN AN AWARD-WORTHY WEBSITE FOR A LIVE WEDDING PAINTER

I need you to design the **UI/UX, visual system, interactions, animations, transitions, and overall digital experience** for a premium website for a **live wedding painter named Shivani**.

The content for the website already exists.

**Do not write, rewrite, invent, or expand the website copy.**

Your job is to figure out **how that existing content should be presented** so the website feels extraordinary.

---

# 1. UNDERSTAND THE BUSINESS CORRECTLY

Shivani is a **live wedding painter**.

She physically attends weddings and **paints the wedding live while the event is taking place**.

She observes:

- the couple
- the ceremony
- guests
- the venue
- lighting
- decor
- emotion
- movement
- important moments

and turns what she sees into an original painting during the wedding.

The final artwork becomes a physical artistic memory of the wedding.

This is therefore NOT:

- a generic artist portfolio
- a digital artist portfolio
- a wedding photography website
- a wedding planner website
- an online art store
- a generic luxury wedding template

The site's central story is:

# A REAL MOMENT → OBSERVED → PAINTED LIVE → BECOMES A MEMORY

The website itself should make the visitor experience this transformation.
